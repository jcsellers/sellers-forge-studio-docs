# Customer Support & Documentation — Sellers Forge Studio

Welcome to the support portal for **Sellers Forge Studio** Atlassian Cloud applications:

1. **Pure-Forge SVG Mermaid & Technical Diagram Studio** (`Confluence` + `Jira`)
2. **Pure-Forge Bi-Directional Markdown Tree Importer, Exporter & Live Macro** (`Confluence`)
3. **Pure-Forge Bulk Label & Taxonomy Governance Manager** (`Jira` + `Confluence`)

## How to Get Support
- **Bug Reports & Feature Requests:** Open an issue at https://github.com/jcsellers/sellers-forge-studio-docs/issues
- **Response Target:** Standard support inquiries are reviewed within 1–2 business days.
- **Security & Trust:** All three apps are 100% Zero-Egress Atlassian Forge applications eligible for the **Runs on Atlassian** program. No customer data ever leaves your Atlassian Cloud tenant.
